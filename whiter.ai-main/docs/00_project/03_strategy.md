# Strategy Brief

## Market Opportunity

The meeting productivity software market is valued at over $4 billion globally and growing rapidly as hybrid and remote work make meeting alignment harder than ever. Real-time AI collaboration tools are one of the fastest-growing B2B SaaS categories in 2025-2026 — but almost all current tools focus on post-meeting summaries, leaving a critical gap: helping attendees follow along *during* the meeting. Whiter.ai is positioned at exactly that gap.

## Target Customer Profile

**Persona: Marcus, 38, Head of Product at a 200-person SaaS company**
Marcus runs 3–5 internal meetings per week — sprint planning, stakeholder syncs, all-hands updates. He's frustrated that attendees zone out, ask questions that were already answered, or leave meetings without a shared understanding of what was decided. He's tried note-taking tools and post-meeting summaries but finds people don't read them. He needs something that works *in the room*, in real time, to keep everyone on the same page.

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | What they do | Their gap |
|---|---|---|
| **Otter.ai** | Real-time speech transcription and post-meeting summaries | Text-only output; no visual boards; doesn't help attendees follow along *during* the meeting |
| **Fireflies.ai** | AI meeting notes, transcripts, and action items | Post-meeting focused; no live visualization; attendees still disengage in real time |
| **Microsoft Copilot (Teams)** | Real-time transcription + AI meeting recap in Teams | Locked to Microsoft ecosystem; produces text summaries, not visual boards; no live audience aid |

## Your Differentiator

Whiter.ai is the only tool that generates live visual boards *during* the meeting — not after it. While every competitor focuses on helping the meeting leader recall what was said, Whiter.ai focuses on helping every attendee understand what's happening right now, turning passive listeners into active participants.

## Landing Page Strategy

**Hero message**: Lead with the attendee problem — "Stop losing your audience mid-meeting." The hero should communicate that Whiter.ai turns spoken words into live visual boards instantly, so every person in the meeting stays aligned and engaged. Show a short auto-playing demo of a board being generated in real time.

**3 Feature cards**:
1. **Live Visual Boards** — AI listens and builds structured visuals as the meeting unfolds, no manual input required
2. **Real-Time Comprehension** — Every attendee sees key points, summaries, and structure appear as they're spoken — no more lost threads
3. **Instant Engagement** — When people can follow along visually, they ask better questions and leave meetings aligned

**CTA**: "Join the waitlist — be first to transform your meetings" — targeting meeting leads who want to be early adopters of a productivity edge.
